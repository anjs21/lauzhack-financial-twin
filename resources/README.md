
# Public Data and Research Resources

**LauzHack 2026 | AI Financial Twin Challenge**

## Purpose

This folder provides publicly accessible data sources, research references, APIs, and technical resources for developing Financial Twins.

The resources can support financial simulation, counterfactual exploration, economic stress testing, decision support, computer games, AI agents, and interactive applications.

A Financial Twin is not inherently a forecasting model. Historical economic data can be used to establish context, calibrate assumptions, construct scenarios, or test sensitivity to changing conditions.

## Swiss Data Sources

### Swiss National Bank — Data Portal
https://data.snb.ch/en

Provides Swiss economic and monetary statistics, including interest rates, exchange rates, inflation-related series, credit, and financial accounts.

### Swiss Federal Statistical Office
https://www.bfs.admin.ch/

Provides official statistics on population, employment, salaries, housing, consumption, education, and social conditions.

### Swiss STAT-TAB
https://www.pxweb.bfs.admin.ch/?px_language=en

Provides downloadable statistical tables for selected Swiss datasets.

### Swiss Open Government Data
https://opendata.swiss/

Catalogue of public-sector datasets from Switzerland.

- Changes in inflation assumptions
- Interest-rate scenarios
- Currency movements
- Salary and employment conditions
- Housing-cost differences
- Household consumption
- Market stress scenarios
- Pension and retirement assumptions
- International relocation
- Economic resilience

## Data Usage Principles

1. Verify the source and publication date.
2. Check licensing and attribution requirements.
3. Distinguish historical observations from assumptions.
4. Document transformations and missing data.
5. Match geographic and temporal coverage.
6. Avoid presenting aggregate statistics as individual financial facts.
7. Do not assume historical relationships will continue.
8. Do not upload confidential or real personal financial records.

## Synthetic and Public Data

The `data/` folder contains fictional financial profiles and transactions.

The `resources/` folder contains links to external public data and research.

Teams may combine synthetic profiles with documented economic assumptions derived from public sources, subject to applicable licensing requirements.
