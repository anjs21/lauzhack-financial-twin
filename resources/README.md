Welcome
This folder provides optional public datasets, free software, open-source technologies, and technical references for participants developing an AI Financial Twin.

All resources are optional. No specific dataset, software, framework, or methodology is required.

Participants may use provided synthetic data, download selected public datasets, generate fictional data, or combine approaches.
Public Data Resources
- [Data catalogue](data/README.md) — downloadable public datasets
- [Swiss economic data](data/swiss-economic-data.md) — Swiss statistics
- [Macroeconomic data](data/macroeconomic-data.md) — GDP, inflation, unemployment, rates and FX
- [Financial markets data](data/financial-markets-data.md) — yields, credit and property prices
- [Household finance data](data/household-finance-data.md) — income, expenditure and wealth
Data entries should state whether links are direct downloads, APIs, or interactive export pages. Check dataset terms before redistribution.

Responsible Use
See [Data Usage and Licensing](data-usage-and-licenses.md).
- Use synthetic or appropriately licensed public data.
- Never include confidential or real personal financial records.
- Check dataset, software, model and asset licenses.
- Document sources, assumptions and limitations.
- Validate financial calculations.
- Do not present simulated outcomes as guaranteed forecasts.
Freedom to Innovate
A Financial Twin may be a simulator, AI assistant, serious game, life simulator, multi-agent environment, causal decision laboratory, optimization engine, virtual world, or hybrid research prototype.

Don't predict the future. Make it explorable.
