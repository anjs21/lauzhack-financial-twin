# Swiss Economic Data

> **Optional resources.** Teams may use the provided synthetic data, select any public dataset, create their own fictional data, or use no external data. No source, technology, method, or interface is required.

A Financial Twin explores *conditional decisions and consequences*, not a guaranteed future. All examples are educational and do not constitute financial advice.

Optional Swiss-specific observations and export tools.

## 1. Swiss consumer price inflation

- **Provider:** World Bank
- **Access:** CSV ZIP
- **Download or export:** https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/CHE/indicator/FP.CPI.TOTL.ZG?downloadformat=csv
- **Optional Financial Twin use:** Stress-test fictional spending and purchasing power
- **Interpretation:** Annual national inflation, not a personal spending index

## 2. Swiss GDP growth

- **Provider:** World Bank
- **Access:** CSV ZIP
- **Download or export:** https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/CHE/indicator/NY.GDP.MKTP.KD.ZG?downloadformat=csv
- **Optional Financial Twin use:** Historical economic environment
- **Interpretation:** GDP growth is not salary growth

## 3. Swiss unemployment

- **Provider:** World Bank / ILO modelled estimate
- **Access:** CSV ZIP
- **Download or export:** https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/CHE/indicator/SL.UEM.TOTL.ZS?downloadformat=csv
- **Optional Financial Twin use:** Employment disruption stress tests
- **Interpretation:** Aggregate rate is not personal unemployment probability

## 4. Swiss population

- **Provider:** World Bank
- **Access:** CSV ZIP
- **Download or export:** https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/CHE/indicator/SP.POP.TOTL?downloadformat=csv
- **Optional Financial Twin use:** Demographic context for multi-agent models
- **Interpretation:** Population total is not household microdata

## 5. Swiss national economic series

- **Provider:** Swiss National Bank
- **Access:** EXPORT INTERFACE (CSV/JSON from selected table)
- **Download or export:** https://data.snb.ch/en
- **Optional Financial Twin use:** Exchange-rate, rates, credit and monetary scenarios
- **Interpretation:** Choose a statistical table; click Download or API to obtain actual series

## 6. Swiss household/earnings statistical tables

- **Provider:** Swiss Federal Statistical Office
- **Access:** EXPORT INTERFACE (choose table and export)
- **Download or export:** https://www.pxweb.bfs.admin.ch/
- **Optional Financial Twin use:** Synthetic household, earnings and cost assumptions
- **Interpretation:** Search Household Budget Survey or Earnings Structure Survey; availability differs by table


## Source and usage checks

Check observation period, units, geographic coverage, data revisions, missing values, and the provider’s licensing/attribution terms. Historical statistics are not individual financial facts or guaranteed future assumptions.
