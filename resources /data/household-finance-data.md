# Household Finance Data

> **Optional resources.** Teams may use the provided synthetic data, select any public dataset, create their own fictional data, or use no external data. No source, technology, method, or interface is required.

A Financial Twin explores *conditional decisions and consequences*, not a guaranteed future. All examples are educational and do not constitute financial advice.

Optional public statistics and survey data; never treat public aggregate values as a specific person’s finances.

## 1. Swiss household budget and earnings tables

- **Provider:** Swiss Federal Statistical Office
- **Access:** EXPORT INTERFACE (choose table)
- **Download or export:** https://www.pxweb.bfs.admin.ch/
- **Optional Financial Twin use:** Calibrate fictional expenses and salaries
- **Interpretation:** Aggregate data; check definitions and availability

## 2. US Survey of Consumer Finances public data

- **Provider:** US Federal Reserve
- **Access:** DATASET DOWNLOAD PAGE (public files)
- **Download or export:** https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/scfindex.htm
- **Optional Financial Twin use:** Experiment with assets, debt and wealth distributions
- **Interpretation:** US households; not representative of Switzerland

## 3. Swiss population

- **Provider:** World Bank
- **Access:** CSV ZIP
- **Download or export:** https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/CHE/indicator/SP.POP.TOTL?downloadformat=csv
- **Optional Financial Twin use:** Demographic context
- **Interpretation:** Not microdata

## 4. Swiss GDP per capita

- **Provider:** World Bank
- **Access:** CSV ZIP
- **Download or export:** https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/CHE/indicator/NY.GDP.PCAP.CD?downloadformat=csv
- **Optional Financial Twin use:** International context
- **Interpretation:** GDP per capita is not disposable income

## 5. European income and living conditions datasets

- **Provider:** Eurostat
- **Access:** EXPORT INTERFACE (select income/living conditions dataset)
- **Download or export:** https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/data/database
- **Optional Financial Twin use:** European household context
- **Interpretation:** Select exact dataset and review coverage


## Source and usage checks

Check observation period, units, geographic coverage, data revisions, missing values, and the provider’s licensing/attribution terms. Historical statistics are not individual financial facts or guaranteed future assumptions.
