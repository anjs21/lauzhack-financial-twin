
# 100 Financial Twin Questions

** LauzHack 2026 | Example Question Library**

These questions are intended to inspire scenario exploration.

They are not predictions, recommendations, or mandatory challenge requirements.

## A. Education and Career

1. What happens if I start working instead of pursuing a master's degree?
2. How would studying abroad affect my savings?
3. Can I afford a two-year master's degree?
4. What happens if I work part-time while studying?
5. How does taking a gap year change my finances?
6. What if I change careers at age 30?
7. How would a promotion affect my long-term goals?
8. What if I accept a lower salary for better work-life balance?
9. How much financial flexibility would I lose by returning to university?
10. What if I become unemployed for six months?

## B. Relocation

11. What if I move from Lausanne to Zurich?
12. How would moving to London change my financial situation?
13. What happens if I work in Germany for three years?
14. Can I afford to move to New York?
15. How do different living costs affect my savings?
16. What happens if I earn in EUR but save in CHF?
17. How would moving abroad affect my financial goals?
18. What if I return to Switzerland after five years?
19. Can I maintain my savings rate while living abroad?
20. How much emergency cash would I need before relocating?

## C. Housing

21. Can I afford to rent an apartment alone?
22. What happens if I move into shared accommodation?
23. What if I buy a home in five years?
24. How would a larger down payment affect my liquidity?
25. What happens if mortgage rates increase?
26. Should I compare renting and buying over ten years?
27. How does property maintenance affect my budget?
28. What happens if my rent increases by 15%?
29. How would moving to a cheaper apartment change my savings?
30. Can I buy a home without sacrificing my emergency fund?

## D. Savings and Liquidity

31. What if I save CHF 300 more every month?
32. How long would it take to build a CHF 20,000 emergency fund?
33. What happens if I stop saving for one year?
34. How much cash would I have after a major purchase?
35. What happens if my expenses increase unexpectedly?
36. How does saving earlier affect my financial flexibility?
37. What if I reduce discretionary spending by 10%?
38. How much liquidity would I need for a career break?
39. What happens if I receive a one-time bonus?
40. How do different savings strategies compare?

## E. Investments

41. What if I start investing at age 20?
42. What happens if I invest CHF 200 monthly?
43. How would increasing contributions affect my financial position?
44. What happens if markets decline sharply?
45. How does my investment horizon affect my options?
46. What if I withdraw investments to finance education?
47. How do investment fees affect different scenarios?
48. What happens if I change my asset allocation?
49. How sensitive are my goals to investment-return assumptions?
50. What happens if I pause investment contributions?

## F. Debt

51. What if I take a student loan?
52. How would paying off debt earlier affect my cash flow?
53. What happens if interest rates increase?
54. Can I afford a personal loan?
55. What if I consolidate multiple debts?
56. How does a payment holiday affect total borrowing costs?
57. What happens if I finance a car with a loan?
58. How does borrowing affect my financial flexibility?
59. What if I prioritize debt repayment over investing?
60. How long would it take to become debt-free?

## G. Family and Lifestyle

61. What happens if I have a child?
62. How would parental leave affect household finances?
63. What if I financially support my parents?
64. How does combining finances with a partner change the picture?
65. What happens if household income decreases?
66. Can I afford a one-year career break?
67. How would a major medical expense affect my savings?
68. What if I increase my travel budget?
69. How would changing my lifestyle affect long-term goals?
70. What happens if I reduce working hours?

## H. Entrepreneurship

71. Can I afford to start a business?
72. What if my startup generates no revenue for one year?
73. How much financial runway would I need?
74. What happens if I leave employment to become self-employed?
75. How would taking a business loan affect personal finances?
76. What if I start a business while working part-time?
77. How do different startup-cost assumptions affect liquidity?
78. What happens if my business fails?
79. How would receiving investment funding change my position?
80. What if I sell my business after five years?

## I. Retirement and Long-Term Goals

81. What if I retire at 55 instead of 65?
82. How does working longer affect retirement resources?
83. What happens if I increase pension contributions?
84. How would a career interruption affect retirement planning?
85. What if retirement expenses are higher than expected?
86. Can I take partial retirement?
87. How would relocating after retirement affect costs?
88. What happens if I begin saving for retirement later?
89. How sensitive is my retirement plan to inflation?
90. What financial trade-offs come with retiring earlier?

## J. Complex and Combined Scenarios

91. What if I pursue a master's degree and move abroad?
92. What if I start a business while repaying student debt?
93. Can I buy a home and still pursue early retirement?
94. What if I lose my job during a market downturn?
95. How would high inflation and lower income affect my goals?
96. What if I move abroad and later return to Switzerland?
97. How do education, career, and housing decisions interact?
98. What sequence of decisions preserves the most flexibility?
99. Which assumptions have the greatest impact on my financial options?
100. What changes would help me meet multiple financial goals?

## Final Note

These questions are starting points.

Students may define new scenarios, combine questions, or develop entirely different approaches.

The value lies in exploring consequences under transparent assumptions.
