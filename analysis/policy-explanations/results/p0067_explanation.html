Level 1 / LLM planner

What did the LLM plan?

Source: Bundled web example (P0067); LLM-generated plan, matched to web/src/example.json. Model: deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash. Version 2.

This is one saved LLM-generated plan. The narrative and reasons are the model’s statements, not evidence of its internal reasoning. The saved versions respond to different user messages; they are not repeated samples of the same input.

Plan narrative

I'll finish my master's and look for a full-time job elsewhere — the simulator already moves me to full-time once I graduate, so a separate step adds nothing. I keep spending lean and only invest cash above a nine-month cushion, because my emergency fund is cash and I won't sell investments in a dip. My partner and I move in together and look for something bigger once we both earn properly. If cash gets thin I cut spending again rather than move to a cheaper place or borrow — and while I'm still studying I accept that cash may dip, because my income jumps once I graduate.

Spontaneity setting: 35.0% (applies to unplanned work and home actions).

What this does and does not show

  • The saved plan is LLM-generated; its narrative and stated reasons are self-reports, not verified causal explanations.
  • The saved draft and revision respond to different messages and are not repeated samples of one state.
  • The opening intervention explains the rule policy conditioned on one state and the fixed saved plan, not the LLM; masks and triggers can change.
  • Exact one-step probabilities omit uncertainty about traits, state and model. Plan-step reports count different events over all paths and are not mutually exclusive.
  • Plan versus habits also changes spontaneity and multiple planned intentions, so it does not isolate individual steps or identify causal effects.
  • Financial uncertainty intervals quantify finite-path simulation error conditional on the model, not real-world forecast uncertainty.
  • Plan and baseline simulate same random seed, but path-dependent behaviour and events may diverge.
  • Saved plans revised as of a later date are simulated from January 2025; the results are counterfactual, not a reconstruction of what was known then.
  • All amounts nominal CHF; goal uses the simulator fixed December 2029 deadline and credit-breach rule.
  • The offer stress changes the simulated world with the plan fixed; it does not ask the LLM to replan.
