How does starting cash change the LLM’s plans?

Live planner experiment · Mia · deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash

Experiment

Repeated drafts at each cash setting, with the same profile and planning wishes. Each draft can include simulation feedback and revision. The saved Mia plan in the other report is not one of these drafts.

Wishes: I want to keep my job, move to a bigger home when practical, and protect my emergency savings.

Cash settings: CHF 3,000 and CHF 12,000 · Samples: 10 per setting · Simulation: 300 paths per draft, same seed 20261122.

Planner marked 10/10 low-cash drafts and 10/10 baseline-cash drafts ready after feedback.

Sampling uncertainty: small sample sizes give wide uncertainty intervals. Counts describe whether a step appears anywhere in a plan; a conditional fallback counts even when it is unlikely to occur. These are not probabilities of actions taken or evidence of causation.

What was sampled?

The same model and wishes were used at both cash values. The comparison changes starting cash in the planner’s prompt and in the simulator feedback. The model may choose different timing, conditions and commitments even when a step’s action type appears in both groups.

The output does not establish what a real person would choose. Results may change with the model, prompt, reference scenario or simulation assumptions.
