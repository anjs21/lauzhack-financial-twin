Four moves, a handful of options each

All four are chosen every month. Masks remove options that make no sense in the current state, and status and housing moves cost money and utility, so the policy has no reason to flip-flop.

Why it is not trivial

Five trade-offs the policy has to learn

  1. Study or start work. Two years on CHF 1,200 plus tuition buy a 50% chance of the next pay level, which is 17–35% more pay for the rest of the horizon. S001 · S011
  2. Work less or retire early. Free time against lost pay. The pension starts only at 65, so stopping earlier must be paid for from C and I. S017 · S091
  3. Housing against goals. One tier is worth CHF 550–780 a month in Bern, and a child forces at least a small flat. S031 · S032 · S082
  4. Lifestyle against saving. Upgrading costs little, but cutting back hurts, so an upgrade should wait for a safe buffer. S041 · S046
  5. Invest or hold cash. A recession raises job-loss risk and cuts returns together, so the cash buffer matters most exactly when the portfolio is down. S061 · S065 · S110
