Interpretation and limitations
  • Study and work are opening intentions, subject to action masks, admission and job offers; later decisions are adaptive. Check the selected-action rates above, especially for non-student profiles.
  • Tuition of CHF 8,000 is a fixed baseline stress assumption, not necessarily the profile's own tuition. It applies to modelled student months and only excess fees are separately charged.
  • The high-priority goal is evaluated using the simulator's fixed December 2029 deadline and credit-breach condition, which may differ from the dataset's deadline.
  • 95% intervals describe Monte Carlo error conditional on the simulator and assumptions, not a range of individual futures, causal effects, or real-world uncertainty.
  • Job-offer multipliers and other stresses are illustrative. Small grid differences may be noise. Synthetic data and personal offsets limit interpretation.
