
Evaluation — AI Financial Twin Challenge
LauzHack 2026 | Proposed, technology-neutral evaluation framework
Status: Draft for organizer approval. This is not an official judging policy until confirmed by the event organizers.

Purpose
Evaluate how effectively a prototype helps a person explore financial decisions under explicit assumptions. A Financial Twin is not defined by forecasting accuracy, an LLM, a dashboard, or a specific technology.

All solution formats are eligible: games, financial simulations, conversational agents, multi-agent systems, causal decision laboratories, reinforcement learning, optimization, immersive experiences, and hybrids.

Guiding principles
- Judge demonstrated capabilities, not named frameworks or model size.
- Credit mathematical rigor and meaningful interactivity equally across different interfaces.
- Do not require forecasting, Monte Carlo, reinforcement learning, LLMs, or any particular stack.
- Reward reproducible, explainable scenario outcomes.
- Treat synthetic or appropriately licensed public data as the default.
- Distinguish calculated results, assumptions, and uncertainty.
- Avoid interpreting a higher simulated net worth as universally the “best” life decision.
Minimum demonstration evidence (proposed)
1. A fictional financial profile and initial state.
2. At least one meaningful intervention or what-if decision.
3. A reproducible before/after or baseline/alternative comparison.
4. Explicit financial assumptions and calculation logic.
5. An understandable way for users to inspect outcomes and limitations.
The challenge: Don't predict the future. Make it explorable.
