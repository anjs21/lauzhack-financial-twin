# FAQ — Challenge and Scope

**LauzHack 2026 | AI Financial Twin**

### 1. What are we building?
A prototype that represents a fictional financial situation and lets a person explore how decisions, events, or assumptions affect possible financial outcomes.

### 2. Is the challenge to predict the future?
No. The focus is conditional **what-if exploration**. Forecasting may be one optional subcomponent, but it does not define the Financial Twin.

### 3. Is a chatbot required?
No. A chatbot, game, dashboard, simulation, agent environment, or other interface may be appropriate.

### 4. Do we have to use artificial intelligence?
The challenge invites AI-enabled approaches, but no particular AI method or model is prescribed. Teams should explain what their chosen intelligence or computational technique contributes. Confirm any formal AI-specific eligibility requirement with organizers.

### 5. Is a digital avatar required?
No. Avatars are optional.

### 6. Must we use Alexia?
No. Alexia (fictional, age 20, Lausanne, CHF 8,000 initial cash) is an illustrative starting point. Teams may create other fictional profiles.

### 7. Do we need to implement all 120 scenarios?
No. The scenario library is an **idea catalogue**, not a required implementation checklist. A carefully implemented and validated scenario is more valuable than many superficial scenarios.

### 8. Can we combine scenarios?
Yes. For example, further education plus relocation plus a change in living costs. Combined effects should be accounted for consistently.

### 9. Can we focus on a specific life stage?
Yes. Education, career, housing, family, entrepreneurship, or retirement can each provide meaningful scope.

### 10. Can we create a game?
Yes. A financial life simulator, branching narrative, strategy game, or immersive experience is a valid approach if actions change an underlying financial state coherently.

### 11. Is financial advice expected?
No. The system should support understanding of assumptions, alternatives, and trade-offs, not make unsupported guarantees or personalized investment recommendations.

### 12. What is a minimum credible demo?
A fictional starting state, at least one intervention, a baseline/alternative comparison, explicit assumptions, and an explanation of the calculated differences.

### 13. Must we build a production-ready application?
The materials describe a hackathon prototype. Any binding requirements are set by organizers.

### 14. Can we build an original approach not mentioned in the documentation?
Yes. The documentation is inspiration, not an exhaustive or prescribed architecture.

### 15. Can we reuse open-source libraries?
Generally yes, subject to their licenses and event rules. Attribute dependencies and verify any model-weight or asset restrictions.
