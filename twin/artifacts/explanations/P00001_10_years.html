How to interpret these plots

PDPs average predictions over observed profile-months; holding other inputs fixed may create implausible combinations. The selected local path is closest to the median terminal net worth and is not necessarily typical in every year. PDP curves compare the observed 2025 and 2026 contexts. The PDP is not a ten-year forecast.

Income and rent use a two-feature Shapley game with mode-specific medians. Employment, city, and month use separate one-feature Shapley comparisons while other inputs remain fixed; those categorical contributions cannot be summed with the income/rent values. Employment compares the path with the initial status, city with the most common profile city, and each month with January. Categorical LIME uses only these two settings, so a high local R² is expected and does not validate the model. LIME approximates expected flow near that path; its weights use a different scale and should not be compared directly with Shapley. Local results hold the later path fixed and exclude personal residuals and other cash movements.

Model-member and perturbation ranges show limited sensitivity, not predictive confidence intervals. The model was trained on synthetic data; these plots show model behaviour rather than established causal effects.

The retained JSON output contains simulation summaries, including uncertainty about simulated outcomes. Negative-balance depth is not an estimate of the initial funding required.
